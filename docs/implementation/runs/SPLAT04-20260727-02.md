## Render and rollback decisions

- `?renderer=gltf` and `?renderer=legacy` are the only accepted renderer flag values.
- `gltf` is the default. A total placement failure safely falls back to `legacy`.
- The GLB visual layer never replaces game simulation or authoritative collision.
- Art review intentionally renders authored static paint. Gameplay does not.
- Gameplay renders `ground-court.glb` 0.075 m below its authored placement so its neutral
  border remains visible while the dynamic `PaintFloor` wins depth testing.
- The shared PMREM is generated in memory because the kit contains no HDRI or texture images.
  Browser/Blender parity is therefore not claimed.

## Known tradeoffs carried into the screenshot gate

- Visual placements come from the provisional GLB layout while authoritative movement,
  muzzle checks and camera collision still use the legacy obstacle footprint. Those shapes
  can visibly diverge. Replacing authoritative collision is outside this milestone and must
  not be implied by successful rendering.
- The loader intentionally requests all 31 primary/LOD GLB variants on mobile as well as
  desktop so release proof and cache warming cover the complete kit. The current kit is
  small enough for the checkpoint, but this trades initial mobile transfer/decode work for
  deterministic completeness and should be revisited after visual approval.

## Screenshot checkpoint correction

The first production gameplay capture loaded all 31 GLBs and reported a ready 94-placement
visual layer, but the playable view was almost black. Bounds checks found three provisional
visual/authoritative-spawn conflicts: cyan `(-28, -4.5)` and `(-28, -1.5)` overlap
`dome-0`, cyan `(-28, 4.5)` overlaps `patio`, and magenta `(28, -1.5)` and `(28, -4.5)`
overlap `dome-3`. The captured player was inside the patio set, while the original yaw also
faced north instead of toward centre court.

The renderer applies camera-obstruction hiding only to GLB bunkers, perimeter, planters and
props intersecting the camera-to-player segment. This is presentation-only: it does not move
assets or players and does not alter authoritative collision.

The next art-review capture proved the flamingo geometry was present but nearly black.
Inspection of `flamingo-fountain.glb` found a `MeshStandardMaterial` named `chrome` with a
light base colour, metalness `1`, roughness `0.06` and no texture. Runtime normalization now
targets only authored chrome-name conventions, raises roughness to `0.16` and environment
intensity to `2.4`, and keeps a neutral light sRGB base. The shared generated PMREM now has
bright reflection-only horizon/card bands while the visible sky retains its original
gradient. This improves readability but does not claim Blender parity without an HDRI or
authored texture/reflection rig.

Initial GLTF gameplay yaw is now resolved once per game mode from the actual player position
to the open centreline target at `(0, 6.5)`. Tests prove the representative cyan and magenta
spawn rays clear their nearest can bounds while the flamingo remains inside the normal field
of view. Legacy rendering, cover, camera mechanics and later player-controlled camera
movement remain unchanged.
